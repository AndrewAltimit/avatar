Asset generation (M4)

avatar-anim-gen emits Unity-YAML assets Unity will accept: .anim clips and FX-layer analog-gesture blend trees. The hard part is correct fileIDs and field names — solved with a faithful hand-written emitter and deterministic, diffable IDs. Full text: docs/reference/anim-gen.md.

What it generates

  • Animation clips (AnimationClip, class 74) — blendshape-weight and GameObject-active curves.
  • Analog-gesture blend trees (BlendTree, class 206) — blending GestureLeft/RightWeight.
avatar anim-gen clip --name Smile --blendshape Body:Smile:100 -o Smile.anim avatar anim-gen blendtree ...

The emitter

A faithful hand-written YAML emitter (yaml_emit) handles Unity's exact field names, block indentation, and flow maps. IdGen hands out deterministic FNV-1a-seeded fileIDs, so generated assets are diffable and reproducible across runs. Every generated clip round-trips through the avatar-unity-yaml reader in tests.
